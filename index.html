About this engineer

Cloud infrastructure engineer building production platforms on Azure and AWS. I'm the technical lead for infrastructure across a team of 9 at SITA, running 8 platform projects over 32 subscriptions in four regions. I built the platform behind SITA's airline messaging services from an empty subscription through to a live cutover off decades-old private cloud infrastructure, and wrote the cross-cloud Terraform modules now used by 25+ engineers. Before that I owned the production reporting and traceability system at Bruss, and moved from the shop floor into software engineering. Full right to work in the UK and EU.

Software Engineer · Bruss

May 2022 – April 2025 · Sligo, Ireland

Completed

Bruss manufactures sealing systems and rubber components for Daimler, BMW, VW, Porsche, Audi, Ford and Land Rover. The Sligo plant runs 24/6 and produces over 150,000 parts a day, with annual output of around €10M. Moved from the shop floor into the plant's small software team, then became its technical lead.

  • Built and owned the plant's production reporting and traceability system end to end. PHP and Python backend services, REST APIs and a React frontend, used daily by 200+ operators, supervisors and quality staff across 14 lines, replacing a mix of spreadsheets and paper records.
  • Scrap rate fell from 15% to 10% over the period the system was in use, and the plant passed its IATF 16949 audit with the system providing lot-level traceability.
  • Administered 30+ enterprise Linux machines end to end, from provisioning through to troubleshooting. Moved builds from hand-configured to Ansible-provisioned, which is the work that pushed me from application development toward platform engineering.
  • Brought the team from FTP-to-production onto Git, peer review, PHPUnit and its first GitLab CI pipeline. Containerised the stack with Docker, which became the foundation of the plant's later cloud migration.
  • Interviewed graduate candidates, ran onboarding, and wrote the plant's engineering onboarding and code review standards.
